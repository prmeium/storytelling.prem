hi, i'm prem.

i tell stories, bring people together, and help good ideas find their first believers.

i’m from ahmedabad. usually somewhere between a camera, a conversation, and an idea i can’t leave alone.

this is the longer version of how i got here.

01 / the obsession

the details have always got me.

the curve of a car. the energy in a crowded room. that one cut in a video that makes you watch it again.

i used to call that feeling a chef’s kiss. i still do.

i love the moment when the timing, the frame, and the story all land. when something stops feeling like content and starts making you feel something.

last year, this page was mostly about holding a camera and telling better stories. that’s still part of me. the canvas has just gotten bigger.

02 / finding my people

then i started bringing people into the same room.

in college, i was drawn to people who were making things happen. creators. founders. people with half an idea and enough energy to try it.

that became the baithak club: a community i founded and grew to 200+ creators and founders.

i worked on the meetups, the partnerships, the unglamorous bits that make a community actually happen. we supported eight student ventures along the way.

it taught me that getting people to show up is a skill. giving them a reason to come back is the real work.

03 / out in the field

good stories need somewhere to go.

i’ve worked across events, partnerships, and growth. from offline acquisition at famapp to building partner networks at project clay. a lot of conversations. a lot of follow-ups. a lot of learning by doing.

most recently, i worked on growth and go-to-market at eduro and spurby.

at eduro, that meant creator campaigns and taking an ai learning product into a room of roughly 70 students. explaining it, watching what clicked, and seeing what people actually tried.

at spurby, it meant researching fashion brands, speaking with founders and operators, and understanding where work gets stuck between teams.

different products. different people. the same question kept showing up:

what would make someone
actually care?

that’s the bit i keep coming back to. the story, the people, and the work it takes to connect them.

04 / november 2026

a new chapter, same curiosity.

i recently wrapped up my role at eduro and spurby. i’m looking for my next early-stage team: growth, partnerships, go-to-market, or founder’s office.

i’m especially interested in work where i can talk to users, shape the story, test an idea, and get something out into the world.

remote works. ahmedabad works. i can start immediately.

and yes, i still want to make things that earn a second watch.
